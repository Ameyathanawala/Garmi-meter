Garmi Meter

How hard is today's heat on your body, and what should you do about it?

👉 Open the tool: [https://ameyathanawala.github.io/Garmi-meter/](https://ameyathanawala.github.io/Garmi-meter/)

Garmi Meter is a free heat-safety tool for India. A weather app tells you the temperature. Garmi Meter tells you what that heat will do to you: in the sun or shade, indoors or out, in your clothes, doing what you are doing.

What it shows
How hot it feels around you. This adds sun, humidity and wind to the air temperature (UTCI outdoors, room temperature indoors).
How long your body can manage. This is a modelled estimate of the time before your core temperature or water loss reaches a limit, looking up to 4 hours ahead.
What to do now. You get clear actions, from "take regular breaks" to "act now", plus how much water to drink.
Warning signs. It lists the danger signs of heat illness and the emergency steps (108 / 112).

It works for adults, older adults, children and babies. For children and babies it gives guidance only, with no time limit.

How to use it
Open the link on your phone (Chrome works best) and allow location, or pick your city.
Choose where you are (sun, shade or indoors), who it is for, what you are wearing and what you are doing.
Read the result, and check again before you step out.

If it feels hotter than the tool says, tap the temperature and enter a reading from your thermometer or weather app.

How it works
Weather: live forecasts from four global weather models (ECMWF, NOAA GFS, DWD ICON, UK Met Office) via Open-Meteo. For each hour the tool uses whichever forecast is the most stressful.
Body: the JOS-3 human thermoregulation model (Takahashi et al., 2021), which tracks core and skin temperature and sweating minute by minute.
Limits: a core temperature of 38 °C, and water loss of 5 % of body weight (3 % if you cannot drink). These follow ISO 7933.
Indoors: a room model estimates the temperature from the walls, roof, windows and fan. You can replace the estimate with your own thermometer reading.
Please note
It is an estimate, not a measurement, and not medical advice. It cannot see your health or your exact surroundings, and forecasts can be several degrees off.
How you feel matters more than any number. If you feel unwell in the heat, stop, cool down and get help.
Privacy: there is no account and nothing is stored. Your location is used only to fetch the weather forecast.
Languages

English, Hindi and Telugu (the Telugu version is a draft under review).
